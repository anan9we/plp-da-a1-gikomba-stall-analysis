## Business Problem

Wanjiku runs a fresh produce stall at Gikomba Market and wants to understand how her business is performing. She needs to analyse 14 days of sales data to identify her best and worst sales days, compare weekly and weekend performance, monitor stock levels, and calculate product gross margins. This analysis uses Python to turn the sales and inventory data into useful information that can help Wanjiku make better business decisions.

## How to Run

Open the notebook in Google Colab:

[Open in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/anan9we/plp-da-a1-gikomba-stall-analysis/blob/main/stall_analysis.ipynb)

## Top Three Findings

1. **Total sales:** The stall generated **KES 46,450** over the 14-day period, with an average daily sale of **KES 3,317.86**.

2. **Weekend performance:** Average weekend sales were **KES 4,900**, which was **82.50% higher** than the average weekday sales of KES 2,685.

3. **Weekly growth:** Sales increased from **KES 22,050 in Week 1** to **KES 24,400 in Week 2**, representing a growth of **10.66%**.
